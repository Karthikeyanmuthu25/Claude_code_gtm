# Customer Discovery Plan

Session ID: `kasparro-ai-visibility-v2`

---

This discovery plan aims to validate the interest and needs of mid-market ecommerce, SaaS, and fintech companies in optimizing their visibility on AI-driven platforms. It seeks to explore existing pain points, measuring systems, and capability gaps to confirm underserved needs and potential demand for more effective solutions.

## Discovery Questions

**Q: Tell me about the last time your brand wasn’t visible in AI-driven search results or assistants. What happened?**

- Tests: Mid-market and enterprise brands are currently not well-represented or optimized for visibility in AI-generated recommendations and searches.
- Listen for: Specific recent instances where their brand was not listed by AI assistants, indicating lack of visibility.

**Q: Describe any strategies you’ve tried to improve your brand’s visibility in AI platforms like ChatGPT. What worked and what didn’t?**

- Tests: The target sectors currently lack sufficient in-house capability or knowledge to optimize for AI visibility without external tools or services.
- Listen for: Lack of well-developed strategies or mention of failed attempts implying capability gaps.

**Q: How do you currently measure the impact of AI assistant visibility on your revenue or customer acquisition metrics?**

- Tests: Improvements in AI visibility can be quantitatively measured and acted upon using metrics like inclusion rates, recommendation frequency, and trust signals.
- Listen for: Mention of quantifiable metrics used or lack of measurement indicating an area for improvement.

**Q: Who in your organization is most involved when dealing with visibility issues on AI platforms, and what actions have they taken?**

- Tests: The target sectors currently lack sufficient in-house capability or knowledge to optimize for AI visibility without external tools or services.
- Listen for: Names of roles or individuals involved; lack of specialized roles may indicate a need for external solutions.

**Q: How frequently do you experience challenges with poor visibility on AI-driven platforms compared to other marketing challenges?**

- Tests: These brands experience or could experience significant revenue impact due to poor visibility in AI-driven channels compared to traditional search engines.
- Listen for: Frequency of challenges occurring, which would indicate the severity and potential impact on revenue.

## Recruiting Channels

**LinkedIn communities for CMOs/VP of Marketing roles in ecommerce, SaaS, and fintech.**

- Why: Target professionals who are decision-makers in relevant industries, active in sharing knowledge and discussing industry trends.
- Evidence: Guidance suggests LinkedIn is a viable platform for targeting specific job roles in precise industries.

**Slack communities like CMO Coffee Talk or other relevant marketing leader groups.**

- Why: These communities facilitate direct engagement with CMO-level participants experiencing AI visibility challenges.
- Evidence: Inferred need for targeting marketing leader communities where buy-in and discussion around marketing challenges are common.

**Industry-specific conferences or virtual summits tailored to ecommerce, SaaS, and fintech sectors.**

- Why: Direct interaction with potential customers who are already focused on enhancing their digital strategy and may face the issues discussed.
- Evidence: Suggested as strong recruiting channels for meeting potential customers aligned with the problem domain.

**Curated newsletters like Martech Today or Chiefmartec.**

- Why: Likely read by VP Marketing/CMOs who are looking at new tech trends impacting marketing at mid-to-large enterprises.
- Evidence: Guidance supports newsletters as useful channels to reach targeted marketing professionals.

## Outreach Message Template

> Hi [Name], I noticed that many in [your industry] face challenges with AI-driven search visibility impacting brand reach. Could you spare 20 minutes to share your experiences? Your insights could help shape new solutions.

## Screening Criteria

- Works at a mid-market company in ecommerce, SaaS, or fintech with global operations.
- Holds a VP Marketing, CMO, or Head of Data/Analytics position.
- Recently experienced visibility challenges on AI-driven platforms.
- Shows interest in improving AI-driven search and assistant visibility.

## Success Criteria

At least 4 of 8 interviewed CMOs/VPs independently recount incidents of poor AI visibility leading to actionable impacts (e.g., loss in leads or sales).

## Recommended Next Steps

1. Prepare outreach messages and identify target profiles on LinkedIn.
2. Join relevant Slack communities like CMO Coffee Talk for deeper engagement.
3. Subscribe to and identify potential contributors from newsletters like Martech Today.
4. Attend or participate in industry-specific events to network with potential interviewees.
5. Conduct the initial set of 10 interviews focusing on past challenges with AI-driven visibility.
