## Search Intent Agent: AI search visibility and GEO tool search terms

**Objective:** Understand how consumers are using AI-driven platforms for product discovery in the target sectors.

### Findings

**Q: Which AI platforms are most frequently used by consumers for product discovery within the target sectors?**

Most frequently used AI-mediated platforms for product discovery today are Google Search (with AI features), Amazon/retail platforms, and ChatGPT, with Google remaining the primary starting point in most markets. Dedicated assistants like ChatGPT are significant but not yet mainstream for product discovery, and Perplexity/Emerging tools are niche and tech-heavy in their audience.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://meetglimpse.com/google-trends/search-volume/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Trends, https://meetglimpse.com/google-trends/

**Q: What are the specific queries or intents that lead consumers to discover products in these sectors through AI systems?**

Specific intents leading to product discovery in AI systems are mainly: Ecommerce/D2C: personalized gift ideas, outfit or product recommendations, category “best” lists, and price-bounded suggestions. SaaS: “best tools” lists, vendor comparisons, evaluations for specific industries/use cases. Fintech: explanation of concepts plus cautious exploration of “best account/card/loan for X,” typically early-stage, not direct application. These are predominantly commercial discovery intents rather than pure purchase-checkout intents; AI is used to narrow options, then users go to websites or traditional search to transact.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://meetglimpse.com/google-trends/search-volume/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Trends, https://meetglimpse.com/google-trends/

**Q: How does AI-driven search impact consumer decision-making processes compared to traditional methods?**

AI-driven search accelerates and shapes early-stage consideration, often providing branded shortlists and comparisons that influence which vendors/products are evaluated. It does not yet consistently replace traditional search, reviews, and direct site visits for final decision-making and purchase, particularly in higher-stakes categories like SaaS and fintech. It can disadvantage brands with weak presence in public, structured, and review-type data, indirectly impacting revenue by excluding them from AI-generated shortlists, but current evidence is mostly qualitative rather than quantified.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://meetglimpse.com/google-trends/search-volume/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Trends, https://meetglimpse.com/google-trends/

### Summary

There is emerging consumer use of AI assistants for product discovery, with more substantial interest among tech-savvy and younger demographics. Queries are generally more informational with some commercial intent, particularly in sectors like SaaS and ecommerce. AI-driven search primarily affects the early decision-making stages by providing shortlists of options but does not yet replace traditional methods for final purchases. This suggests a moderate opportunity for businesses to leverage AI visibility in early consumer interactions, though traditional channels remain pivotal.

**Opportunity signal:** Moderate
