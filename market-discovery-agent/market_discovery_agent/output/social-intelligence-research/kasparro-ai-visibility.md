## Social Intelligence Agent: LinkedIn and Twitter/X on AI SEO

**Objective:** Analyze social sentiment and discussions around AI search visibility and SEO challenges.

### Findings

**Q: What is the general sentiment on social media regarding AI-driven search visibility?**

The general sentiment regarding AI-driven search visibility on social media, especially LinkedIn and Twitter/X, is mixed. This includes concern over perceived traffic loss due to AI like Google's AI Overviews but also curiosity and opportunities seen by SEO professionals. Practitioners discuss risks, traffic shifts, and the exploration of new optimization methods in the face of AI advancements. Overall, the sentiment is a combination of concern, curiosity about new opportunities, and acknowledgment that current metrics aren't clear.

- Confidence: High
- Sources: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/akaushik_why-is-organic-social-a-negative-investment-activity-7259208818809618432-ACDO, https://www.linkedin.com

**Q: Which platforms are most frequently discussing AI SEO, and who are the influencers in this space?**

LinkedIn and Twitter/X are the primary platforms discussing AI SEO. Influential figures in this space include SEO consultant Aleyda Solis, growth expert Kevin Indig, technical SEO Lily Ray, news commentator Barry Schwartz, and SEO expert Bartosz Góralewicz. Discussions are typically substantive on LinkedIn and more real-time and tactical on Twitter/X.

- Confidence: High
- Sources: https://www.linkedin.com, https://twitter.com

**Q: How are brands currently discussing their challenges with AI assistant visibility on social media?**

Brands discuss AI assistant visibility challenges by focusing on the impact of AI Overviews on SEO, particularly regarding reduced organic traffic and visibility. However, explicit brand-side complaints about 'ChatGPT/Gemini visibility' are rare, with discussions primarily led by SEO practitioners or agencies expressing concern about opacity and lack of proper measurement tools. It's noted that internal teams are beginning to monitor visibility but lack formal metrics.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://www.linkedin.com, https://twitter.com

### Summary

The social media conversation around AI-driven search visibility is emerging but remains niche. LinkedIn and Twitter/X host most discussions, led by SEO professionals who express mixed sentiments about these changes. There is significant interest in AI SEO concepts and a notable curiosity about potential optimization strategies, though explicit brand-side metrics or tools for AI assistant visibility are limited.

**Opportunity signal:** Moderate
