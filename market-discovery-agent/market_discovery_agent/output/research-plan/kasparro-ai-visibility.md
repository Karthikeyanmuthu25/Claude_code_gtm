## Industry Agent

**Objective:** Understand the current state of AI search engine optimization and its growth potential.

**Questions:**
- What are the latest trends in AI-assisted shopping and consumer decision-making?
  *Why:* Understanding these trends will validate whether brands truly prioritize AI visibility, aligning with the hypothesis that AI assistants are critical in consumer decision-making.
- How prevalent are AI-specific toolsets compared to traditional SEO tools?
  *Why:* This information will help confirm or refute the hypothesis that there are few AI visibility toolsets compared to traditional SEO.
- What industry challenges are currently associated with AI search engine optimization?
  *Why:* Knowing the challenges will assess if Kasparro's features align with industry pain points, as hypothesized.

**Suggested sources:** Gartner, Forrester, CB Insights

## Competitor Agent

**Objective:** Identify existing competitors and their solutions addressing AI search visibility.

**Questions:**
- Which companies currently offer AI search visibility solutions, and what features do these solutions include?
  *Why:* Identifying competitors can confirm the uniqueness of Kasparro's offerings and fill any gaps in toolset availability.
- How are competitors' products priced, and what business models do they use?
  *Why:* Understanding pricing will help assess market demand and willingness to pay, which is critical for Kasparro's commercial strategy.
- What strategic partnerships do these competitors leverage to enhance their AI SEO capabilities?
  *Why:* Identifying partnerships will indicate how Kasparro might secure endorsements or partnerships to validate its approach and enhance credibility.

**Suggested sources:** Crunchbase, PitchBook, Owler
