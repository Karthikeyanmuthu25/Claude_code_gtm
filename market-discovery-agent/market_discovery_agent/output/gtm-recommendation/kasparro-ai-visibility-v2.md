# GTM Recommendation

Session ID: `kasparro-ai-visibility-v2`

---

## Primary Motion: Founder-led LinkedIn content + AI visibility audit offer  *(confidence: Low)*

The GTM plan focuses on leveraging founder-driven LinkedIn content and a free AI visibility audit as lead-generating mechanisms to tap into the nascent interest in AI-driven product discovery optimizations among mid-market SaaS, ecommerce, and fintech companies. Given the current weak demand for AI visibility solutions and unproven category traction, starting with a validation-driven motion is recommended, targeting specific pain points and testing the market's response before scaling further. This approach balances demonstrating value and educating the market while gathering crucial validation signals.

## Positioning Statement

> For mid-market ecommerce, SaaS, and fintech companies struggling with AI search visibility, our platform uniquely optimizes your AI-driven product placement to boost high-intent query inclusion and recommendation frequency.

## Messaging Pillars

- Increase measurable share of high-intent AI queries through expert visibility techniques.
- Quantitative benchmarks and diagnostics in AI visibility to drive GTM KPIs like pipeline lift and conversion rates.
- Strategic guidance to navigate and leverage AI search platforms effectively, filling in-house capability gaps.
- Built-in data insights that align AI optimization efforts with concrete sales and marketing outcomes.

## Primary Channels

### Founder LinkedIn content focusing on AI visibility challenges

- Why: Establishes the founder as a thought leader in AI-driven product search, building credibility and generating leads.
- Evidence: External research highlights LinkedIn as effective for GTM-related outreach and content-led engagement, especially for new categories.
- First actions:
  1. Develop and publish 5 LinkedIn posts addressing AI visibility challenges and solutions in ecommerce, SaaS, and fintech.
  2. Connect with 50 new mid-market marketing leaders and initiate engagement through thoughtful commenting.
  3. Message engaged LinkedIn connections directly to offer personalized visibility insights and discussions.
  4. Host a live LinkedIn session discussing trends and insights from AI search optimization research.

### Free AI visibility audit as a lead magnet

- Why: Free audits are a proven entry strategy for similar GTM and consulting tools, offering initial value and prompting ongoing engagement.
- Evidence: GTM playbooks recommend audits as an entry to showcase initial value and transition to a subscription or services offer.
- First actions:
  1. Create an audit template that assesses current AI visibility and proposes quick wins.
  2. Design a landing page offering the free AI visibility audit to capture interested leads.
  3. Promote audit offer through LinkedIn posts and industry Slack/channel engagements.
  4. Target LinkedIn/Slack group messages to key marketing personas about the audit offer.

## Pricing & Packaging

Offer a free AI visibility audit leading to a tiered annual subscription model: entry at $10k/year for mid-market, scalable features at $25k/year for enterprise, with a focus on value-metric-driven packaging based on seats and usage.

## Launch Sequence

1. Develop and publish initial LinkedIn content series tackling issues and solutions in AI visibility.
2. Design and launch a dedicated landing page for the free AI visibility audit with a clear CTA.
3. Engage LinkedIn and Slack communities to promote and discuss the audit offer.
4. Conduct initial audits with early leads, and document results and feedback.
5. Follow up with personalized demos showcasing potential improvements and ROI.
6. Adjust messaging based on feedback and insights from early interactions.
7. Analyze early conversion metrics from audit signups and LinkedIn engagement.
8. Refine audit process and outcome presentations for ongoing conversions.
9. Expand outreach to additional marketing decision-makers within the target sectors.

## Metrics to Track

- Number of LinkedIn engagements, comments, and connection requests from target roles.
- Conversion rate of audit landing page visitors to signups.
- MQL-to-SQL conversion rate from audit signups to demo requests.
- Feedback score and qualitative insights from initial audit feedback.
- Tracking uplift in AI-generated shortlists post-audit among early user base.

- Weak existing demand for AI visibility solutions could limit initial lead volume.
- Complexity in conclusively tying audit insights to tangible economic outcomes.
- Channel saturation on LinkedIn could diminish content engagement if not executed distinctly.
- Strategic uncertainty and reluctance to invest in AI-specific optimization within target sectors.
