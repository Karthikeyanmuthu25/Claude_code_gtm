## Overall Confidence: Low

The market for AI search visibility tools is currently nascent, lacking specific published market-size figures. Using the SEO software market as a proxy, the TAM is estimated at $14.6B-$74.6B annually, reflecting global estimates for the closely related SEO software sector. Narrowing this to B2C industries in North America and Europe, the SAM ranges from $3.5B-$12.5B annually. Due to the early developmental stage and competition, the SOM is conservatively estimated at $100M-$200M over three years, assuming a 1-2% market capture. Overall confidence in these estimates is low, reflecting the speculative nature of this emerging market segment.

- Focus on mid-market and enterprise companies in North America and Europe using relevant technographics

**How SAM was narrowed from TAM:** Narrowed using ICP firmographics: B2C eCommerce and Retail, Consumer Brands, Travel industries in North America and Europe, companies with 200+ employees using CRM systems like Salesforce and marketing automation tools.
