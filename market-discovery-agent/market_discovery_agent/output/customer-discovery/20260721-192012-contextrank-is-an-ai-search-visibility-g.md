# Customer Discovery Plan

Session ID: `20260721-192012-contextrank-is-an-ai-search-visibility-g`

---

This discovery plan seeks to validate whether early-stage B2B SaaS founders are aware of and affected by AI engines (like ChatGPT) citing competitors, impacting their lead generation. We're exploring channels such as dedicated SaaS and tech startup forums to recruit informed founders for interviews, aiming to surface genuine use cases and pain points that a solution could address.

## Discovery Questions

**Q: Tell me about the last time you noticed being left out of AI-generated recommendations or citations compared to competitors. What impact did this have?**

- Tests: Founders discover/recognize this problem primarily via a triggering event (seeing a competitor cited by an AI engine).
- Listen for: Listen for specific instances where founders realized they were not being cited by AI, and if this realization led to identifiable negative impacts like losing prospects or deals.

**Q: Describe the tools or methods you currently use to try and manage your LinkedIn visibility or AI citation presence. What works and what doesn’t?**

- Tests: Founders already use methods or seek tools for AI citation visibility, indicating a pre-existing awareness and need.
- Listen for: Validation would be mention of specific tools or processes used, dissatisfaction with current solutions, or interest in dedicated solutions.

**Q: Walk me through how you first heard or realized that AI engines might be impacting your lead conversion in favor of competitors.**

- Tests: Founders can recall instances of AI engine influence on lead conversion or citation of competitors over themselves.
- Listen for: Founders sharing anecdotes or expressing realizations that align with AI engines impacting their market presence, even if indirectly.

**Q: Have you ever had a conversation with a lead where they mentioned learning about a competitor through an AI engine? What was the outcome?**

- Tests: A meaningful number of early-stage B2B SaaS founders can point to losing a conversation to a competitor due to AI engine citation.
- Listen for: Direct mention of leads citing AI engines as their source for competitors and any resultant business impact.

**Q: If you had a tool that improved your AI citation (like a free audit), would this be something you actively use or invest in? Why or why not?**

- Tests: Founders will voluntarily submit their LinkedIn URL and email for a free audit if it seems beneficial.
- Listen for: Willingness to use such a tool if perceived benefits are clear, reflecting potential interest and utility.

## Recruiting Channels

**r/SaaS subreddit**

- Why: This community focuses on software as a service topics and can include potential founders experiencing the described problem.
- Evidence: Secondary research pointed to broader SaaS communities discussing AI visibility issues, making them a logical starting point for recruiting.

**SaaS Growth Hacks Facebook Group**

- Why: It hosts SaaS founders discussing growth, marketing, and operational challenges including lead generation and visibility.
- Evidence: The group often features discussions around SaaS marketing tactics, which aligns with the founder's target segment.

**Indie Hackers Forum - AI/ML Category**

- Why: Here, tech founders and entrepreneurs frequently share and discuss the impacts of new AI/ML technologies, possibly including citation issues.
- Evidence: External research suggested niche communities focus more deeply on issues relevant to individual operators, offering actionable insight.

## Screening Criteria

- Founder or CEO of a seed to Series A B2B SaaS company.
- Current focus on marketing/sales tech or related sectors.
- Primarily operates in North America or Europe.
- Leverages LinkedIn for personal branding or lead generation.
- Has experienced or is investigating AI citation challenges.

## Success Criteria

At least 3 of 10 interviewed founders independently mention realizing AI engines cited competitors, impacting their lead funnel.
