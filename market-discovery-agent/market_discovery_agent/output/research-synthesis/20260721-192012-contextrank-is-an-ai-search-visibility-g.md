## Recommendation: Pivot needed
**Opportunity score:** 45/100

The opportunity to help B2B SaaS founders optimize their LinkedIn profiles for AI citation appears underdeveloped. Evidence shows that AI engines are indeed influential in B2B vendor discovery, but the causal relationship between LinkedIn profile changes and AI citation frequency lacks robust support. Furthermore, there's no reliable data showing founders have lost deals due to AI engines recommending competitors. These gaps suggest limited market validation and signal a need for pivoting to fortify the business case.

## Key Strengths

- Industry reports highlight LinkedIn as a valuable professional citation source in AI engines.
- No direct competitors currently focusing on founder-level AI citation across LinkedIn.

## Key Risks

- Lack of primary evidence that founders perceive AI engine citation as a revenue-impactful problem.
- No established research demonstrating the causal relationship between LinkedIn profile optimization and increased AI citations.

## Critical Open Questions (primary research needed)

- Do early-stage B2B SaaS founders experience or recognize losing deals due to AI citation gaps?
- Would founders pay for a service to optimize for AI citation if the value/impact is uncertain?
- Is there sufficient founder interest in testing LinkedIn optimizations for AI citation improvement?
