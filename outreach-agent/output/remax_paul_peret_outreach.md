## Company Overview

**RE/MAX Holdings (NYSE: RMAX)** is one of the world's largest real estate franchise networks, operating across 110+ countries with approximately 140,000 agents globally. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado, the business model is a scaled franchise operation — RE/MAX sells franchise rights to brokers, earns continuing royalties on agent activity, charges annual dues, and provides brand infrastructure, technology, and training support. Their Motto Mortgage subsidiary extends the franchise model into adjacent financial services.

**Operational Reality in 2025–2026:**
The business is navigating structural pressure from multiple directions simultaneously:

- **Rate environment headwinds** — elevated mortgage rates suppressed transaction volume industry-wide, compressing royalty revenues tied to agent productivity
- **Competitive disruption** — eXp Realty built a virtual, asset-light brokerage model with dramatically lower overhead; Compass is positioning technology as the core differentiator; both are actively recruiting from RE/MAX's agent base
- **Franchise model stress** — the same infrastructure that scaled RE/MAX to 140,000 agents now creates operational rigidity, making rapid adaptation difficult
- **Technology expectations gap** — agents and franchisees expect enterprise-grade digital tools; closing that gap across a distributed global network is an execution challenge at scale

RE/MAX is not a failing business. It is a dominant brand operating in a market that has fundamentally shifted underneath it — which is exactly the environment where operational leadership matters most.
