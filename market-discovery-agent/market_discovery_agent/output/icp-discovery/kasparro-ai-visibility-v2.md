# ICP Discovery

Session ID: `kasparro-ai-visibility-v2`

---

## Overall ICP Confidence: Medium

This ICP focuses on mid-market companies in the ecommerce, SaaS, and fintech sectors that are seeking to enhance their visibility in AI-driven searches. Typically ranging from 250 to 2,500 employees with $10M–$100M+ in revenue, these companies already invest in CRM and marketing automation but lack specific tools for AI visibility in search assistants. Key decision-makers include CMO or VP Marketing with input from data/analytics leads. Purchase triggers include annual planning cycles or tech stack optimizations, particularly when addressing declines in digital acquisition metrics.

## Primary ICP

### Mid-market companies in ecommerce, SaaS, and fintech  *(confidence: Medium, fit score: 80/100)*

**Market:** AI search visibility optimization

**Industry:** Ecommerce, B2B SaaS, Fintech

**Firmographics:** Mid-market (250-2,500 employees, $10M–$100M+ revenue), global operations

**Technographics:** Typically employ CRM systems like Salesforce or HubSpot, marketing automation platforms like Marketo, and analytics tools like Google Analytics. Often lack specific tools for AI visibility in assistant platforms.

**Buyer persona:** CMO or VP Marketing in collaboration with Head of Data or VP Analytics, who have budget authority and feel the pain of decreased visibility.

**Pain points:** Lack of visibility in AI-driven search results, which impacts traffic and sales.

**Buying signals:** Tech stack optimization cycles, notably during annual planning phases, strategic shifts to AI initiatives, and when noticing declines in digital acquisition metrics. Budget likely exists within existing digital marketing allocations.

**Evidence:** Research indicates moderate opportunity, existing tools in tech stacks lack AI visibility functions; external validation through similar categories and purchase processes exists through Search Intent and Industry Agents.

**Fit score rationale:** Segment strongly matches with current investments in digital marketing technology and existing needs for AI-driven search optimization tools. Scoring driven by size, budget, and alignment with markets identified in secondary sources.

## Exclusion Criteria — Who NOT to Target

Small businesses under 250 employees due to budget constraints, companies without existing investment in digital marketing tooling, those with strong internal AI expertise already.

## ICP Scoring Rubric

- **Company has 250-2,500 employees** (weight: 25) — Check LinkedIn or Apollo data for employee count.
- **Uses a recognized CRM like Salesforce or HubSpot** (weight: 25) — Check company profile for CRM systems in use via technographic tools.
- **Has a marketing automation platform like Marketo** (weight: 20) — Verify through technographic data or company case studies/references.
- **Posted a job or made a strategic shift related to AI initiatives in the last 6 months** (weight: 15) — Review job postings on LinkedIn or press releases/company updates.
- **Annual revenue is $10M–$100M+** (weight: 15) — Assess financial data from business reports or databases.

## Recommended Validation Steps

1. Conduct interviews with CMOs in mid-market ecommerce or SaaS companies about AI visibility concerns.
2. Test willingness to pay by showcasing product prototypes to marketing leaders in these firms.
3. Attend industry conferences to gather informal insights from fintech marketing executives.
4. Survey mid-market firms to determine current spending and perceived need of AI visibility tools.
5. Create a targeted campaign to gauge interest in AI search optimization solutions among identified firms.
