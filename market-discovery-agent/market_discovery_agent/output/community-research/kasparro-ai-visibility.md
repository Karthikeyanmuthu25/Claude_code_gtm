## Community Agent: SEO and marketing forums on AI search visibility

**Objective:** Gauge community-driven insights and developer interest related to AI search visibility tools.

### Findings

**Q: What discussions are occurring within marketing and SEO communities about AI search engine optimization?**

Users are organically discussing how to be cited in Google’s AI Overviews/SGE and not lose traffic, and how to get mentioned by ChatGPT and similar tools when users ask for product/tool recommendations. These discussions are mostly confused about how ranking/inclusion works, and short on concrete, repeatable playbooks. Vendor content amplifies the topic but often rebrands traditional SEO advice as 'AI SEO,' lacking user-backed proof.

- Confidence: High
- Sources: (none found)

**Q: Are there grassroots or open-source projects aimed at AI search visibility?**

There are some small, mostly experimental projects that detect whether AI Overviews appear and which URLs are cited (Google only). There are many more projects that use AI to generate content for SEO, but do not address visibility in AI assistants. There is no robust open-source system that tracks AI assistant inclusion across ChatGPT, Gemini, etc., nor a community standard for 'AI recommendation metrics.' This gap suggests your idea is not yet commoditized.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: (none found)

**Q: What feedback do early adopters or testers of similar tools provide, and how positive is this feedback?**

For tools tracking AI Overviews / SGE, users like awareness of where AI is cannibalizing clicks and seeing whether they are cited. However, users are unsure how actionable the data is and whether they can really influence inclusion. Overall sentiment is curious but cautious, with high interest in better measurement and clearer levers.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: (none found)

### Summary

The SEO and marketing communities are actively discussing the challenges of AI search visibility, particularly regarding inclusion in AI-generated answers and the impact on traffic. However, concrete playbooks or tools to address these issues are lacking, though there's organic curiosity. There is an absence of grassroots or open-source projects specifically measuring visibility in AI assistants, suggesting an opportunity for new solutions like Kasparro. Current feedback on existing tools is cautiously optimistic, acknowledging awareness benefits but seeking more actionable insights.

**Opportunity signal:** Strong
