## AthenaHQ

- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/company/athena-hq
- Website: https://www.athenahq.ai

### Findings

**Q: What is their existing solution — what does the product actually do?**

AthenaHQ offers an AI work assistant / copilot focused on day-to-day workflows rather than just chat. It includes capabilities for AI-assisted workflow planning, drafting, and execution, as well as workflow automation and collaboration features, mainly benefiting operations, finance, consulting, and support roles.

- Confidence: High
- Sources: https://www.athenahq.ai

**Q: How do they position themselves (category, tagline, core value prop)?**

AthenaHQ positions itself as an "AI work assistant for teams" and a "teammate for repetitive work." The core value proposition is to help teams complete repetitive work faster with structured, reliable workflows driven by AI instead of manual effort.

- Confidence: High
- Sources: https://www.athenahq.ai

**Q: What is their pricing model and roughly what do they charge?**

AthenaHQ does not list specific prices on their website; pricing seems sales-led with a 'Contact' / 'Request access' approach, likely on a per seat or per team/company plan basis, but no specific pricing numbers are available.

- Confidence: Low
- Sources: https://www.athenahq.ai

**Q: Who are their customers (segment, company size, notable named customers)?**

AthenaHQ targets B2B teams in roles dealing with repetitive processes such as operations, finance, support, and consulting. The focus is on SMB to mid-market companies, although specific customer names are not publicly disclosed.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://www.athenahq.ai

**Q: What are their weaknesses — gaps, complaints, or things reviewers criticize?**

AthenaHQ’s weaknesses include a focus on internal workflows instead of external AI search visibility, a need for differentiation in a crowded AI assistant market, lack of transparent pricing, and limited presence on review platforms. No direct user reviews or complaints are cited.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://www.athenahq.ai

### Summary

AthenaHQ is a young tech company that offers an AI assistant to improve team productivity through structured workflows, mainly benefiting knowledge work roles. They focus on internal processes rather than AI-driven external visibility. While they have some traction, their lack of transparent pricing and limited presence in customer review platforms pose challenges. They are currently positioned as solutions for repetitive operational tasks within B2B environments, and do not present a direct threat or alignment to products aimed at improving external AI search visibility.
