**Q: How do industry regulations affect GST reconciliation processes in India for mid-size manufacturers?**

GST regulations require detailed transaction-wise reporting and are subject to frequent changes, complicating compliance. Recent regulatory changes like GST 2.0 and reduced e-invoicing thresholds increase the need for accurate and timely reconciliation, creating checkpoints before filing returns and introducing stricter enforcement for ITC mismatches.
