## Funding Agent: GEO/AEO-tooling funding rounds (Profound, Otterly, Peec)

**Objective:** Assess investor appetite and funding trends in the GEO/AEO space to calibrate the competitive-window hypothesis and understand capital available to a well-funded entrant that could out-build this niche.

### Findings

**Q: How much funding have GEO/AEO startups (Profound, Otterly.ai, Athena, Scrunch AI, Goodie AI, etc.) raised in the last 18 months, and at what valuations/stage?**

Profound has raised $155M between Aug 2024 and Feb 2026, at a $1B+ valuation. Scrunch AI raised $26M pre-acquisition by Sitecore in June 2026. Athena/AthenaHQ has raised roughly $2–3M in seed funding around June 2025. Otterly AI's funding is unclear; reports conflict between bootstrapped or $5M seed. Goodie's funding is not disclosed. Other players like Bluefish AI raised $68M and Peec AI $29.1M in total.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://sacra.com/c/profound/, https://softcircles.com/blog/best-funded-ai-visibility-startups, https://www.marketingtechnews.net/news/athena-raises-2m-to-track-how-brands-appear-in-ai-responses/, https://everything-pr.com/capital-behind-ai-communications

**Q: Are any funded GEO players explicitly targeting individual personal-brand/creator-level tracking, and what do their job postings or public statements suggest about roadmap direction?**

No funded GEO player is explicitly targeting individual personal-brand/creator-level tracking. Profound and others remain focused on enterprise/brand-level visibility. Athena’s LinkedIn outreach integration is the closest, geared towards brand use cases.

- Confidence: High
- Sources: https://scrunch.com/faqs/which-aeo-platforms-have-the-most-public-case-studies-showing-measurable-impact, https://www.marketingtechnews.net/news/athena-raises-2m-to-track-how-brands-appear-in-ai-responses/, https://everything-pr.com/capital-behind-ai-communications, https://capston.ai/geo-tools/

**Q: What funding or investor interest exists specifically in 'LinkedIn optimization' or 'personal branding SaaS' tools (Taplio, Shield, Supergrow), and are any pivoting toward AI visibility?**

No confident answer found. Information on funding or investor interest specifically for Taplio, Shield, and Supergrow, and their pivots towards AI visibility was not available in excerpts.

- Confidence: Low
- Sources: (none found)

**Q: Is there VC thesis content specifically framing 'AI search visibility for individuals/founders' as an investable category, and if so who is writing checks?**

The broader category of AI search visibility/GEO is an investable category with big VC firms involved. However, no specific VC thesis on 'AI search visibility for individuals/founders' was found in the sources reviewed.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://softcircles.com/blog/best-funded-ai-visibility-startups, https://everything-pr.com/capital-behind-ai-communications, https://sacra.com/c/profound/

### Summary

Funding activity in the GEO/AEO space is robust with substantial capital flowing into brand-focused platforms like Profound and Scrunch, which have raised significant sums. However, there is no clear evidence that any funded Geo platforms are specifically targeting personal-brand/creator-level tracking, particularly around LinkedIn. Traditional personal branding SaaS tools are not documented in the available data, leaving the niche relatively uncrowded at this time.

**Opportunity signal:** Moderate
