# Customer Discovery Plan

Session ID: `gst-reconciler-test`

---

This discovery plan targets finance leaders in mid-size Indian manufacturing companies to validate hypotheses around persistent GST reconciliation issues and automation interest. It seeks in-depth insights into their reconciliation workflows, difficulties, and the potential impact of automation tools, settling the question of a compelling business case for new technology.

## Discovery Questions

**Q: Can you walk me through the last time you handled GST invoice reconciliation against purchase orders?**

- Tests: Mid-size manufacturing companies in India have a significant pain point around GST invoice reconciliation, which affects their compliance accuracy and leads to financial penalties.
- Listen for: Details about difficulties encountered, time taken, errors or mismatches, and any compliance issues that arose.

**Q: What challenges do you typically face with matching GST invoices to purchase orders during month-end closures?**

- Tests: Finance teams currently rely on manual processes or spreadsheets for GST reconciliation, resulting in significant inefficiencies and errors.
- Listen for: Mention of manual processes, use of spreadsheets, specific inefficiencies, or errors frequently occurring.

**Q: How do you currently solve these reconciliation issues? What tools or processes are used?**

- Tests: The tool's ability to automatically flag mismatches and suggest resolutions significantly reduces time spent by finance teams on reconciliation tasks.
- Listen for: Current tools used, whether attempts at automation have been made, and the perceived effectiveness of those tools.

**Q: Tell me about any recent GST audits or penalties your company experienced due to reconciliation errors.**

- Tests: Mid-size manufacturing companies in India have a significant pain point around GST invoice reconciliation, which affects their compliance accuracy and leads to financial penalties.
- Listen for: Incidents of penalties, audits, or reviews highlighting reconciliation errors.

**Q: What factors do you consider important when deciding to invest in improving your GST processes?**

- Tests: These companies are willing to pay for a tool that reduces the time and errors associated with month-end GST filing, as this directly impacts their financial reporting efficiency.
- Listen for: Insights into budget considerations, compliance priorities, and openness to investing in new technologies.

- Why: LinkedIn allows direct access to professional profiles matching the ICP, facilitating targeted recruitment of potential interviewees with relevant roles and responsibilities.

## Outreach Message Template

> Hi [Name], I noticed your expertise in finance for manufacturing. I'm researching how mid-size manufacturers handle GST invoice reconciliation. I'd love to learn about your current process and any challenges faced. Would you have 20 minutes for a call? This is not a sales pitch—just trying to understand the pain better.

## Screening Criteria

- CFO, Head of Finance, or GST Compliance Lead role
- Company size between 200-2,000 employees
- Multi-plant operations in India
- Active involvement in GST reconciliation processes
- Recent experience with GST audits or reconciliation penalties

## Success Criteria

At least 4 of 8 interviewed finance leaders affirm routine inefficiencies or errors in GST reconciliation unprompted; 3 express a proactive interest in automating this process.

## Recommended Next Steps

1. Conduct targeted LinkedIn searches for relevant professionals and send outreach messages.
2. Schedule and conduct interviews with qualifying respondents, focusing on recent reconciliation experiences.
3. Analyze responses for patterns of manual process inefficiency or interest in automation.
4. Refine hypotheses based on gathered insights and determine if the need for a new tool is significant.
5. Plan a secondary outreach phase to gather further validation if initial results are mixed or inconclusive.
