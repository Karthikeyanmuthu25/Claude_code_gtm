## Industry Agent: AI-driven search and recommendation systems in ecommerce, D2C, SaaS, and fintech

**Objective:** Understand the current landscape and trends regarding AI-driven search and recommendation systems in the target sectors.

### Findings

**Q: What are the current trends in AI-driven search and recommendation systems specifically in ecommerce, D2C, SaaS, and fintech sectors?**

There is a shift from traditional '10 blue links' to AI-mediated answers with significant adoption of AI-driven features, such as Google's AI Overviews, which influence over 84% of all search queries. This is impacting click-through rates for traditional search results. Moreover, the usage of generative and agentic AI is increasing among enterprises but is mostly in pilot phases. Consumer use of AI assistants is rising, but evidence does not conclusively show they have overtaken traditional search channels for product research.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: BrightEdge research, McKinsey

**Q: How are companies in these sectors currently addressing visibility challenges in AI-driven platforms?**

Companies are adapting SEO strategies to account for AI Overviews and AI-driven search results by structuring content for AI citation rather than just search ranking. There is some investment in AI capabilities for marketing, but no standard practices or widespread dedicated programs specifically for AI assistant visibility optimization have emerged.

- Confidence: Low
- Sources: BrightEdge research, Polaris Market Research, McKinsey

**Q: What are the key challenges businesses face in gaining visibility on AI platforms, as stated in industry reports?**

Key challenges include a loss of organic visibility and attribution in AI search with fewer clicks on traditional SEO results, capability gaps in AI and data skills, data quality and trust issues in AI systems, and immature measurement frameworks for AI visibility. Strategic uncertainty and reluctance to invest in AI-specific optimization are also notable challenges.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: BrightEdge research, McKinsey, UNCTAD

### Summary

AI-driven search and recommendation systems are becoming integral in ecommerce and related sectors, with AI elements significantly altering traditional SEO landscapes. While companies are attempting to adapt SEO strategies for AI features, there is little evidence of standardized approaches or dedicated strategies specifically for visibility in AI assistants like ChatGPT. Key visibility challenges include declining organic search visibility due to AI features and capability gaps within organizations.

**Opportunity signal:** Moderate
