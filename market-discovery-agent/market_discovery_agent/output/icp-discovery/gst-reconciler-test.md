# ICP Discovery

Session ID: `gst-reconciler-test`

---

## Overall ICP Confidence: Medium

Target mid-to-large mid-size Indian manufacturers with 200-2,000 employees and multi-plant operations who experience significant GST reconciliation issues due to high invoice volumes. Focus on firms using sophisticated ERP systems like SAP/Oracle/NetSuite and experiencing GST penalties or month-end delays. Engage with CFOs or Heads of Finance who control the budget, and GST Compliance Leads who experience the pain firsthand. Avoid smaller manufacturers with less complex operations and limited budget.

## Primary ICP

### Mid-to-Large Mid-size Indian Manufacturers  *(confidence: Medium, fit score: 75/100)*

**Market:** GST compliance software

**Industry:** Manufacturing

**Firmographics:** Companies with 200-2,000 employees, multi-plant operations in India

**Technographics:** Typically use ERPs like SAP/Oracle/NetSuite and supplemental AP tools (e.g., Coupa), possibly reliance on Excel for reconciliation tasks.

**Buyer persona:** CFO or Head of Finance holds budget, while Head of Accounts or GST Compliance Lead experiences the pain.

**Pain points:** Inaccurate and labor-intensive GST invoice reconciliation leading to compliance issues and financial penalties.

**Buying signals:** Receipt of GST audit findings or penalties, month-end close delays, or ERP/AP system upgrades.

**Evidence:** Industry agents highlight widespread GST reconciliation issues among Indian businesses, with mid-market manufacturers likely affected ('Industry Agent', 'External Research'). The buyer search behavior shows persistent interest in GST software ('Search Intent Agent').

**Fit score rationale:** The segment's acute pain and budget, along with ERP and AP tool usage, suggest a strong fit. Pain points and buying signals align well but lack specificity on invoice vs PO reconciliation.

## Exclusion Criteria — Who NOT to Target

Very small manufacturers (<100 employees) with limited budgets and reliance on manual processes; companies primarily using non-GST-compliant tools or lacking a structured finance department.

## ICP Scoring Rubric

- **Company has 200-2,000 employees** (weight: 25) — Use LinkedIn/company websites to verify employee count.
- **Uses ERP systems like SAP/Oracle/NetSuite** (weight: 20) — Check technographic data from LinkedIn or platforms like BuiltWith.
- **CFO or Head of Finance listed in company leadership** (weight: 15) — Verify through LinkedIn or company leadership pages.
- **Recent ERP/AP system upgrade or GST penalty/audit notice** (weight: 20) — Search for news articles or industry reports indicating recent system changes or legal notices.
- **Head of GST Compliance or Indirect Tax listed in their finance team** (weight: 20) — Use LinkedIn to check key finance roles.

## Recommended Validation Steps

1. Conduct interviews with Heads of Finance in mid-size manufacturing firms to discuss GST reconciliation processes.
2. Engage with ERP/AP system vendors to understand their integration challenges and gaps.
3. Survey finance professionals in manufacturing about their current reconciliation methods and pain points.
4. Test outreach to CFOs and Heads of Accounts to gauge their interest in specialized GST tools.
5. Present the product's value proposition to procurement teams managing ERP upgrades to assess compatibility concerns.
