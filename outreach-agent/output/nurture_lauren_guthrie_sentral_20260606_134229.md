There's a third pressure point in asset transitions that rarely gets named but almost always causes timeline compression: physical improvements.

Legal handoffs are contractual. Systems onboarding is procedural. But physical improvements — the construction scope that actually delivers the Sentral experience — live at the intersection of contractor timelines, permitting unpredictability, and the fixed date when residents need to move in.

The operators I work with address this by building what I call a Transition Performance Framework: a single coordination structure that maps legal, physical, staffing, systems, and experience workstreams against shared milestones — with early warning triggers when any workstream threatens the critical path.

It doesn't eliminate construction variability. But it makes that variability visible early enough to adapt rather than react.
