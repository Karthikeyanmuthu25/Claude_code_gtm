# Research Synthesis & Opportunity Assessment

Session ID: `kasparro-ai-visibility-v2`

---

## Recommendation: Pivot needed
**Opportunity score:** 40/100

While there is growing interest in AI-driven search tools, the current evidence suggests that mid-market and enterprise businesses in ecommerce, D2C, SaaS, and fintech have not yet fully prioritized visibility in AI platforms like AI assistants for product discovery. Agents consistently report low existing visibility and strategic capability gaps, but there is weak demand for solutions specifically targeting AI optimization, as well as a lack of compelling funding and community interest. Without clear evidence of strong current market need or high willingness to invest, significant market education and validation work would be needed to solidify the business case.

## Hypothesis Assessments

**[Mixed]** AI assistants (e.g., ChatGPT, Perplexity) are becoming primary channels for product research among the target markets' customers.

Industry and Search Intent agents found increased use of AI assistants, but traditional search tools still dominate. Consumer reliance on AI assistants as a primary channel for product research is growing but hasn't surpassed traditional methods.

*Evidence from: Industry, Search Intent*

**[Supported]** Mid-market and enterprise brands are currently not well-represented or optimized for visibility in AI-generated recommendations and searches.

The Industry agent notes little evidence of standardized SEO practices for AI assistant visibility. The presence of AI visibility-targeted hires is low according to the Job Market agent.

*Evidence from: Industry, Job Market*

**[Mixed]** These brands experience or could experience significant revenue impact due to poor visibility in AI-driven channels compared to traditional search engines.

The Search Intent agent suggests potential impact via exclusion from AI shortlists, but the magnitude is unquantified. Customer actions remain closely tied to traditional search for conversions, complicating revenue impact assessment.

*Evidence from: Search Intent*

**[Refuted]** There exists a strong willingness among target companies to invest in services that measure and optimize AI visibility, assuming they can see concrete improvements in brand recognition and sales.

Community Agent findings show no active, strong demand or conversation about AI optimization, indicating weak current willingness to invest.

*Evidence from: Community*

**[Untested]** Improvements in AI visibility can be quantitatively measured and acted upon using metrics like inclusion rates, recommendation frequency, and trust signals.

The existing findings did not address quantitatively measuring AI visibility improvements with specific metrics.

*Evidence from: (none)*

**[Supported]** The target sectors currently lack sufficient in-house capability or knowledge to optimize for AI visibility without external tools or services.

Job Market and Industry agents report few explicit in-house roles focusing on visibility optimization for AI systems, indicating a skills gap.

*Evidence from: Job Market, Industry*

## Key Strengths

- Emerging consumer use of AI assistants for product discovery.
- Increasing interest in optimizing technology for AI platforms by some sectors.

## Key Risks

- Lack of significant market recognition or priority in the business community for AI search optimization.
- Weak existing willingness from target companies to invest in AI visibility solutions.
- Absence of strong, quantitative evidence or customer case studies demonstrating the revenue impact of AI optimization.

## Critical Open Questions (primary research needed)

- How much are companies willing to pay for AI visibility services and under what conditions?
- What are the key performance indicators target companies expect from AI visibility solutions?
- What detailed use cases or success stories exist that demonstrate baseline improvements and ROI from AI visibility enhancements?
- How do target companies currently conceptualize AI's role in their consumer's buying journey?

## Recommended Next Steps

1. Conduct primary research to validate market interest in AI visibility solutions, focusing on key decision makers in ecommerce, SaaS, fintech.
2. Explore opportunities to educate potential market on the benefits and business impact of AI visibility optimization.
3. Develop case studies or partner with initial clients to establish baseline effectiveness and ROI for potential clients.
4. Consider shifting focus to insights and measurement tools that assist existing SEO strategies rather than exclusively targeting 'AI visibility.'
5. Engage in market surveillance to monitor shifts in AI adoption and investment in AI for commercial discovery.
