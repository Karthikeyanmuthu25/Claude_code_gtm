## Industry Agent

**Objective:** Understand the scale of GST reconciliation issues faced by mid-size manufacturing companies in India and their current practices.

**Questions:**
- What is the prevalence of GST reconciliation issues among mid-size manufacturing companies in India?
  *Why:* This will help validate whether the pain point is significant and widespread, supporting the initial hypothesis.
- How do industry regulations affect GST reconciliation processes in India for mid-size manufacturers?
  *Why:* Understanding regulatory pressures is crucial for assessing compliance and financial penalty risks, which underpin the problem statement.
- What is the typical size and structure of finance teams in mid-size manufacturing companies?
  *Why:* Determining the resources available for such tasks will help understand the scope of reliance on manual processes.
- What trends are emerging in the financial tech solutions space for manufacturing companies in India?
  *Why:* This can guide positioning the tool within the existing industry landscape and anticipating future directions.

**Suggested sources:** Industry reports on GST in manufacturing, Government publications on GST compliance, Interviews with industry experts, Business chambers like FICCI or CII
