## Community Agent: The research explored Indie Hackers, Reddit communities like r/SaaS, r/startups, Hacker News, public LinkedIn posts, Medium articles, and specific consultancy websites like Monckai. Most mentions of AI visibility issues in founder discussions were not user-originated but framed by consultants and marketers.

**Objective:** Find direct, unprompted evidence in founder communities of the core felt-urgency claim: founders noticing/losing deals to AI-engine competitor citations, and how they currently talk about or cope with this.

### Findings

**Q: Are there threads in founder communities where founders mention noticing ChatGPT/Perplexity citing a competitor instead of them, or losing a deal because of it?**

Evidence of generic AI visibility issues exists, mostly in marketing/consultant posts. No evidence found of founders explicitly reporting lost deals because ChatGPT/Perplexity cited a competitor instead. Where AI-related deal loss appears, it is due to AI-generated proposal errors, not competitor recommendation.

- Confidence: Low
- Sources: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/taniadevi_most-b2b-tech-companies-are-losing-deals-activity-7419917852968640512-iB44, https://monckai.com/field-notes/chatgpt-recommends-competitors-not-you, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/halifax-sales-ai_weak-positioning-loses-deals-before-the-demo-activity-7483143468219916289-FiVO, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/karthickraajha_a-founder-i-know-lost-a-deal-in-march-and-activity-7480867370291339264-1dCE

**Q: How do founders currently describe/frame the 'AI search visibility' problem in their own words?**

The problem is currently framed mostly by consultants and tools using terms like 'AI Visibility' and 'generative visibility.' Founders in media talk more about generic AI content concerns and reputational risk, rather than on AI citation frequency. No evidence of founders independently using GEO/AEO terms.

- Confidence: Low
- Sources: https://monckai.com/field-notes/chatgpt-recommends-competitors-not-you, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/taniadevi_most-b2b-tech-companies-are-losing-deals-activity-7419917852968640512-iB44, https://developmentcorporate.com/saas/linkedin-is-now-the-1-ai-search-source-what-saas-founders-must-do-before-their-next-ma-deal/

**Q: What complaints or requests exist about Taplio, Shield Analytics, or similar tools regarding their inability to address AI/LLM discoverability?**

No real, current user complaints or requests found explicitly about Taplio or Shield Analytics not addressing AI/LLM discoverability. Any claims of gaps in AI visibility are suggested in vendor content, but not by the user voice.

- Confidence: Low
- Sources: https://monckai.com/field-notes/chatgpt-recommends-competitors-not-you

**Q: Do B2B SaaS founders discuss third-party corroboration signals (press, G2 reviews, backlinks, Reddit mentions) as things they consciously manage for AI visibility, or is this entirely off their radar?**

Consultants treat third-party corroboration signals as key levers for AI visibility. No clear public evidence found that typical B2B SaaS founders explicitly manage these signals for AI visibility; they use them for general credibility and reputation management.

- Confidence: Low
- Sources: https://monckai.com/field-notes/chatgpt-recommends-competitors-not-you, https://authoritytech.io/curated/linkedin-content-ai-citations-competitor-recommendations-2026, https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/linkedin-ai-visibility-study

### Summary

While the AI search visibility issue receives attention, it is primarily from vendors framing the problem and not yet a mainstream concern among founders in public forums. For the specific problem of losing deals because AI cites competitors, no organic evidence from founders was found. This suggests awareness and urgency for this specific aspect might be lower than anticipated.

**Opportunity signal:** Weak
