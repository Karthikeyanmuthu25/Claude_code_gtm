# ICP Discovery

Session ID: `20260721-192012-contextrank-is-an-ai-search-visibility-g`

---

## Overall ICP Confidence: Medium

This product should target Seed to Series A B2B SaaS founders in marketing and sales technologies who struggle with AI visibility. These founders often act as the primary public-facing role and control budget decisions to resolve visibility challenges, particularly when the pain of being out-cited by AI solutions is acutely felt. Ideally, focus on firms that use LinkedIn as a key acquisition channel and have established budgets for tools that tangibly impact inbound leads.

## Primary ICP

### Seed to Series A B2B SaaS Founders in Marketing/Sales Tech  *(confidence: Medium)*

**Firmographics:** Seed to Series A B2B SaaS companies with 10-200 employees in software/IT, marketing tech, and sales tech, primarily in North America and Europe.

**Buyer persona:** Founder/CEO holds primary LinkedIn presence and visibility pain, also holds budget authority up to mid-4-figures annually for tools.

**Pain & triggers:** Founders express pain as 'AI engines citing competitors instead of me,' especially upon discovering competitors are favored in AI-generated recommendations, triggering the need to rectify their own citation visibility.

**Willingness to pay:** Expected budget for tools of this nature ranges from $30-$200/month for solo founders to $200-$800/month for teams, if the tool directly impacts inbound pipeline visibility.

**Evidence:** External benchmarks and secondary findings (e.g., Industry Agent, Search Intent Agent) indicate overlapping needs among early-stage SaaS firms for tools that enhance AI citation visibility, with specific interest from founders managing their personal branding and discovery on LinkedIn.

## Anti-ICP — Who NOT to Target

Companies where founders are not the public-facing figure (e.g., Series B+ where the company brand supersedes the founder's presence), industries outside B2B SaaS (e.g., consumer tech), or where budgets and team size fall below the capacity to invest in specialized tools (e.g., pre-seed teams under 10 employees).

## Recommended Validation Steps

1. Conduct interviews with founders who actively use LinkedIn for business development to verify specific instances where AI citation has impacted their inbound leads.
2. Organize surveys targeting this segment to quantify the prevalence and severity of AI citation losses.
3. Run a pilot offering free LinkedIn audits with a feedback loop to gauge interest and relevance of audit insights in improving their visibility.
4. Engage with startup accelerators and communities directly (e.g., Entrepreneurial Spark, Y Combinator) to access founders who could report AI citation issues.
5. Develop partnerships with LinkedIn optimization tools to co-market and test feature integrations focused on AI visibility challenges.
