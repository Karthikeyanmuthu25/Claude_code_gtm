## Industry Agent

**Objective:** Understand the current landscape and trends regarding AI-driven search and recommendation systems in the target sectors.

**Questions:**
- What are the current trends in AI-driven search and recommendation systems specifically in ecommerce, D2C, SaaS, and fintech sectors?
  *Why:* Understanding industry trends helps confirm if reliance on AI search is indeed growing across these specific sectors.
- How are companies in these sectors currently addressing visibility challenges in AI-driven platforms?
  *Why:* Identifying existing solutions and strategies helps assess the innovation gap and the potential value of new solutions.
- What are the key challenges businesses face in gaining visibility on AI platforms, as stated in industry reports?
  *Why:* Knowing common challenges can validate the hypothesis about market opportunity due to poor optimization in AI channels.

**Suggested sources:** Gartner, Forrester, McKinsey, Industry-specific publications

## Competitor Agent

**Objective:** Identify which companies are effectively gaining visibility within AI-driven search and recommendation systems in the target sectors.

**Questions:**
- Which competitors are leading in AI visibility within ecommerce, D2C, SaaS, and fintech sectors?
  *Why:* Identifying leaders provides benchmarks for success and insights on best practices.
- What strategies or technologies are these leading companies employing to improve AI visibility?
  *Why:* Understanding the competitive landscape helps identify potential partnership or differentiation opportunities.
- Are there case studies or examples where competitors have successfully optimized for AI-driven channels?
  *Why:* Documented success stories can support the hypothesis of a measurable impact through optimized AI visibility.

**Suggested sources:** Crunchbase, CB Insights, Competitor websites and press releases

## Community Agent

**Objective:** Gauge the perceived value and challenges of AI optimization within professional circles of target sectors.

**Questions:**
- What are the key discussion points or concerns among professionals regarding AI-driven product search and discovery?
  *Why:* Understanding community discussions can reveal the current sentiment and interest level in AI visibility challenges.
- How frequently do professionals within these sectors discuss optimization for AI platforms on forums like Reddit and LinkedIn?
  *Why:* High frequency might correlate with a higher need for solutions that improve AI visibility.
- What solutions or tools are being recommended within these communities?
  *Why:* Identifying existing solutions discussed by the community can highlight gaps or validate the proposed solution's necessity.

**Suggested sources:** Reddit industry subreddits, LinkedIn professional groups, Quora discussions

## Search Intent Agent

**Objective:** Understand how consumers are using AI-driven platforms for product discovery in the target sectors.

**Questions:**
- Which AI platforms are most frequently used by consumers for product discovery within the target sectors?
  *Why:* Identifying key platforms helps focus optimization efforts effectively where it matters most.
- What are the specific queries or intents that lead consumers to discover products in these sectors through AI systems?
  *Why:* Understanding consumer behavior and search intents is crucial for creating targeted optimization strategies.
- How does AI-driven search impact consumer decision-making processes compared to traditional methods?
  *Why:* Examining this impact validates the hypothesis about AI being a primary channel for product research and discovery.

**Suggested sources:** Google Trends, Statista, Market research reports

## Job Market Agent

**Objective:** Determine current hiring trends and skills demand related to AI visibility optimization.

**Questions:**
- What roles are target sector companies hiring for specifically related to AI and machine learning optimization?
  *Why:* Hiring trends can reveal gaps in in-house capabilities and validate the need for external solutions.
- Which skills are most in demand for optimizing presence on AI-driven platforms in these sectors?
  *Why:* Identifying required skills helps understand whether current market capabilities meet the needs for optimizing AI visibility.
- Are there any job postings explicitly mentioning visibility optimization for AI systems?
  *Why:* Mentions in job postings can indicate priority areas for companies and validate the need for AI visibility services.

**Suggested sources:** LinkedIn Job Postings, Glassdoor, Indeed

## Social Intelligence Agent

**Questions:**
- What is the general sentiment of consumers towards shopping decisions influenced by AI platforms in these sectors?
  *Why:* Consumer sentiment analysis helps confirm the increasing reliance and trust in AI-driven recommendations.
- How do consumers perceive brands that are prominently featured in AI-driven recommendations?
  *Why:* Understanding perception towards AI-prominent brands can highlight the potential brand equity benefits.
- Are there common complaints or praises related to AI-driven product discovery in social media channels?

**Suggested sources:** Twitter sentiment analysis, Facebook group discussions, Social media monitoring tools
