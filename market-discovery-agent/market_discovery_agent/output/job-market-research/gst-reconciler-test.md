## Job Market Agent: Naukri, Indeed job postings

**Objective:** Determine the demand for professionals skilled in GST reconciliation within mid-size manufacturing companies in India.

### Findings

**Q: What job roles are commonly associated with GST reconciliation processes in mid-size Indian manufacturing companies?**

Common job roles associated with GST reconciliation include Accounts Executive / Accounts Officer, Senior Accountant / Accounts Manager, GST Executive / Taxation Executive, Finance Executive / Finance Manager, and Indirect Tax roles. These roles often list GST reconciliation among their responsibilities.

- Confidence: High
- Sources: https://www.naukri.com, https://www.indeed.co.in, https://dev.to/nathanhamlett/the-7-hiring-signals-that-predict-budget-before-the-job-posting-goes-up-12d0

**Q: How frequently do these companies recruit for finance roles that specifically handle GST processes?**

There is high overall demand for GST-capable finance staff, with postings on Naukri and Indeed showing continuous recruitment across various finance and accounts roles. Exact frequency per company is unclear, but the roles often emphasize GST knowledge as mandatory.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://signals.gitdealflow.com/signals/hiring-burst

**Q: What skills and experience are listed as requirements for GST-related finance roles?**

Skills required include GST return filing, knowledge of GST forms, GST law knowledge, reconciliation skills, vendor follow-up, experience with ERP/financial software, and advanced Excel skills. Roles typically require 2-7 years of GST experience in manufacturing contexts.

- Confidence: High
- Sources: https://www.naukri.com, https://www.indeed.co.in

### Summary

Job market data suggests a stable demand for finance roles handling GST reconciliation within Indian mid-size manufacturing firms. These roles form part of broader finance responsibilities and require significant expertise in GST processes, suggesting a business opportunity for improved reconciliation tools.

**Opportunity signal:** Strong
