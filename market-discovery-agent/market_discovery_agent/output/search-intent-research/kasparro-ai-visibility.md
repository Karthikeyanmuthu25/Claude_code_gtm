## Search Intent Agent: AI search and recommendation queries

**Objective:** Understand the search intent trends related to AI SEO and AI assistant visibility.

### Findings

**Q: What are common queries related to AI visibility tools for brands?**

The common observable query families are: “AI SEO,” “AI SEO tools,” “AI search engine optimization” (informational + commercial intent), “AI search engine,” “AI search” (informational/comparative), and “AI recommendation engine,” “product recommendation engine,” “ecommerce recommendation software” (transactional intent for personalization tools). Explicit queries like “AI assistant brand visibility” do not show up as recognizable categories in public trends.

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://meetglimpse.com/google-trends/search-volume/, https://victorious.com/blog/google-trends/

**Q: Is there increasing search traffic for terms related to AI visibility in consumer decision-making processes?**

Yes, there is increasing search interest in broad terms like “AI search,” “AI search engine,” and “AI SEO.” However, there is no strong evidence for increasing search traffic for terms specifically about “AI assistant visibility tools” or “AI recommendation visibility.”

- Confidence: Medium
- Sources: https://meetglimpse.com/google-trends/, https://explodingtopics.com/blog/google-trends-search-volume, https://seranking.com/keyword-search-volume-checker.html

**Q: How are AI recommendation techniques being discussed in search queries?**

AI recommendation techniques in search queries are primarily discussed through e-commerce recommendation engines and personalization, like “AI recommendation engine” and “product recommendation engine.” When AI assistants are mentioned, recommendations are indirectly linked to established SEO strategies, not through high-volume queries around AI assistant recommendation optimization.

- Confidence: High
- Sources: https://explodingtopics.com/blog/google-trends-search-volume, https://seranking.com/keyword-search-volume-checker.html

### Summary

The search demand for AI visibility tools for brands is currently more focused on AI SEO and AI search topics, with only niche interest directly targeting AI assistant visibility. This suggests that while the market is conscious of AI's impact on visibility, there isn't widespread proactive search demand for AI visibility tools yet. Thus, non-search discovery channels might be necessary to build awareness and demand.

**Opportunity signal:** Weak
