Session ID: `kasparro-ai-visibility-v2`

## Overall Confidence: Low

The TAM for AI search visibility optimization is estimated between $10 billion and $20 billion, grounded as part of the broader SEO and martech segments. Narrowing to companies fitting the ICP firmographic and technographic criteria yields a SAM of $4 billion to $6 billion. The achievable 3-year SOM is more conservative at $100 million to $300 million, reflecting the nascent demand and strong competitor positioning noted in prior analytical phases.

### TAM: $10B-$20B annually  *(confidence: Low)*

**Timeframe:** current annual market size

**Methodology:** Top-down estimate based on positioning within the broader SEO and martech categories. Given that detailed SEO and martech segments are large (multi-billion USD) but lack precise breakouts for niche categories, I estimated a TAM reflecting a specialized subsector.

**Key assumptions:**
- Positioned as a specialized subsegment of SEO and martech.
- Multi-billion global markets with high single-digit CAGR.
- Current market nascent but aligned to emerging AI visibility needs.

### SAM: $4B-$6B annually  *(confidence: Low)*

**Timeframe:** current annual market size

**Methodology:** Narrowed from TAM using firmographic/technographic filters for mid-market companies in ecommerce, SaaS, and fintech.

**Key assumptions:**
- Refocused TAM on mid-market companies (250-2,500 employees, $10M-$100M revenue) in ecommerce, B2B SaaS, and fintech.
- Use of CRMs like Salesforce or HubSpot and marketing platforms like Marketo.

**How SAM was narrowed from TAM:** Industry: Ecommerce, SaaS, Fintech; Employee Count: 250-2,500; Revenue: $10M-$100M+; CRM system Salesforce/HubSpot and Marketo as marketing automation platform.

### SOM: $100M-$300M over 3 years  *(confidence: Low)*

**Timeframe:** 3-year obtainable revenue

**Methodology:** Bottom-up estimate assuming 1% to 5% capture rate from SAM, reflecting limited current demand highlighted in Phase 3 findings and the presence of early competitors like Profound and Evertune AI.

**Key assumptions:**
- 1% to 5% capture rate based on moderate entry barriers and existing competitors like Profound.
- Assumes significant market education and validation needed.
- Low current market readiness as inferred from Phase 3 recommendations.

**Why this capture rate is realistic:** The SOM assumes a cautious capture rate due to the nascent nature of market demand described in Phase 3, combined with the need for extensive market education. Competitors like Profound and Evertune AI are already positioned, suggesting initial ground presence is needed but is not yet robust or fully capitalized on by a dominant player.
