## Founder Input

**Product idea:**
Kasparro is an AI Visibility Platform that helps brands become discoverable, recommended, and selected by AI search engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and Microsoft Copilot -- continuously measuring AI visibility, diagnosing why a brand is or isn't recommended, and providing a structured remediation playbook to improve inclusion across AI systems.

**Vision:**
Become the operating system for AI recommendation visibility -- the Ahrefs/Semrush/SimilarWeb equivalent for the AI search ecosystem, tracking AI inclusion, recommendations, brand citations, AI trust signals, and buying influence as AI assistants replace traditional search in the buying journey.

**Features:**

- AI Visibility Audit -- inclusion rate, competitive visibility, brand mentions, recommendation frequency, category presence
- Search Visibility Module -- measures whether the brand appears in high-intent prompts (inclusion rate, competitive recall, surface coverage, tracked AI surfaces)
- Trust Signals Analysis -- verified claims, third-party citations, structured proof, schema quality, citation strength
- Content Quality Analysis -- catalog completeness, attribute density, structured metadata, embedding readiness
- Transaction Readiness -- pricing clarity, availability, agent-readable information, site latency
- Prompt Explorer -- lets users enter prompts and see which brands AI recommends, ranking, category responses, competitor inclusion (an AI search simulator)
- Category Intelligence -- tracks AI visibility across categories: supplements, skincare, electronics, apparel, beauty, fitness, nutrition, home, sleep, personal care
- AI Visibility Benchmarking -- compares brands against competitors via an Inclusion Index and other benchmark metrics
- Remediation Playbook -- structured plan to fix issues identified across all audit modules

**Assumptions:**

- AI assistants are becoming the first stop in the buying journey
- Being recommended by AI will become as commercially important as ranking on Google
- AI recommendations depend on structured, trustworthy, machine-readable information rather than traditional SEO alone
- AI visibility can be measured objectively through metrics such as inclusion rates, citations, trust signals, and recommendation frequency
- Brands can systematically improve AI recommendations by fixing structural issues instead of relying on prompt engineering ("durable positioning beats prompt hacks")
- Continuous measurement is essential -- optimization requires ongoing monitoring rather than one-time improvements ("measurement precedes acceleration")

**Founder knowledge:**
Deep expertise in AI search and LLM behavior: how AI systems retrieve, synthesize, and recommend brands across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot.
Strong technical SEO background: structured data, schema, entity optimization, catalog architecture, machine-readable content.
Information retrieval expertise: search indexing, knowledge graphs, entity relationships, AI retrieval mechanisms.
E-commerce and product catalog experience across categories like supplements, skincare, electronics, apparel, beauty, fitness, nutrition, home, sleep, and personal care -- product attributes, merchant feeds, commerce data quality.
Revenue operations focus -- product and messaging built around commercial/revenue outcomes rather than vanity SEO metrics.
Experience building AI measurement and benchmarking systems: inclusion, recommendation frequency, citation quality, category coverage, AI trust signals.

## Phase 1 Output

### Problem Statement

Brands increasingly rely on AI assistants as a key channel for driving consumer discovery and purchasing. However, they struggle to understand how their visibility and recommendation status on AI search engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, and others are determined, making it difficult for them to ensure their inclusion and optimize their presence. Without clear visibility metrics and actionable steps, these brands risk missing out on crucial consumer touchpoints and losing competitive advantage in AI-driven search environments.

### Initial Hypotheses

1. Brands actively seek out AI search visibility because they acknowledge AI assistants are critical to consumer decision-making.
2. There is a lack of toolsets in the market that specifically address AI search inclusion and recommendation, unlike traditional SEO tools for web search.
3. Brands can meaningfully influence their AI recommendation status through changes in structured data, content quality, and trust signals.
4. AI assistants use quantifiable metrics such as inclusion rate and recommendation frequency, which can be consistently measured and improved.
5. The proposed features of Kasparro align with the biggest visibility challenges brands face when dealing with AI search engines.

### Success Criteria

1. Successful pilot studies with key brands demonstrating a measurable increase in AI search visibility indices (e.g., inclusion rate, recommendation frequency) after using Kasparro tools.
2. Strong engagement from target brands, indicated by the sign-up and active usage metrics for the platform's tools and audits.
3. Feedback from early adopters that Kasparro's strategic insights directly led to improved competitive positioning and increased consumer interactions via AI recommendations.
4. Market research or customer validation demonstrating demand and willingness to pay for AI-specific visibility solutions among marketing and SEO teams.
5. Securing partnership or endorsements from AI search engine platforms, validating Kasparro's methodology and algorithms.
